# Typography system

Source section: [03 — The Type System: Fonts for Neubrutalism](https://neubrutalism.com/#typography)

Typography is neubrutalism's primary graphic force. Build contrast between an expressive display face, a structural heading face, a restrained body face, and an optional monospace face that signals exposed or engineered structure.

## Role architecture

| Role | Primary use | Desired character | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Oversized headlines, hero statements, brand-defining moments, high-impact calls to action | Heavy, tightly tracked, poster-like | Use selectively; do not use for paragraphs or routine labels |
| Heading | Section headings, card titles, navigation | Distinctive at medium sizes, readable, structurally firm | Organize content without competing with display intensity |
| Body | Paragraphs and sustained reading | Calm, conventional, predictable, generously spaced | Restraint is a functional requirement |
| Monospace | Code-like material, labels, tokens, metadata | Mechanical, deliberate, engineered | Use as a semantic accent, not a body replacement |

Create contrast through role, scale, and weight rather than decorative letterforms. Keep the most extreme typography in headlines, heroes, and high-value actions.

## Display faces

| Typeface | Stated weights | Classification | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Syne | 400–800 | Quirky grotesque | Assertive geometry with enough idiosyncrasy to feel intentional; used by the source site |
| Bebas Neue | 400 only | Condensed display | Narrow poster energy and strong vertical presence |
| Archivo Black | 400 only | Heavy grotesque | Maximum mass for composition-dominating headlines |

Choose Syne for geometry plus character, Bebas Neue for condensed poster force, and Archivo Black for sheer mass. Do not extend these faces into sustained reading merely for consistency.

## Heading faces

| Typeface | Stated weights | Classification | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Space Grotesk | 300–700 | Geometric sans with quirk | Structural clarity with personality; used by the source site |
| Plus Jakarta Sans | 200–800 | Modern geometric | Clean warmth that can temper a sharper display face |
| Outfit | 100–900 | Round geometric | Broad flexibility and a rounder counterpoint to maximal display type |

Navigation belongs to the heading role because it needs personality plus medium-size legibility, not poster-scale aggression.

## Body faces

| Typeface | Stated weights | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| Inter | 100–900 | Neutral, screen-oriented utility face; used by the source site |
| DM Sans | 100–1000 | Warmer and more geometric while remaining readable at paragraph sizes |

Choose Inter for maximum neutrality and DM Sans when body copy should retain more warmth. A broad available weight range does not justify using every weight in one system.

## Monospace faces

| Typeface | Stated weights | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| Space Mono | 400, 700 | Geometric, mechanical, visibly aligned with the style; used by the source site |
| JetBrains Mono | 100–800 | High legibility, broad range, and code-oriented utility |

Choose Space Mono when structural labels should participate in the visual personality. Choose JetBrains Mono when dense technical legibility matters more. Reserve monospace for content that benefits from an engineered signal.

## Recommended pairings

| Pairing | Display | Heading | Body | Monospace | Character |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Source-site system | Syne 800 | Space Grotesk 700 | Inter 400 | Space Mono | Expressive geometry with a neutral reading layer |
| Condensed and warm | Bebas Neue 400 | Plus Jakarta Sans 700 | DM Sans 400 | JetBrains Mono | Narrow poster force balanced by warmth |
| Maximum mass | Archivo Black 400 | Outfit 700 | Inter 400 | Space Mono | Heavy display presence with clean structural contrast |

Pairing-specific monospace weights are not prescribed. The families and weights are curated examples, not universal requirements. Equivalent families may substitute when licensing, script coverage, localization, platform availability, or brand identity requires it; preserve the role contrast.

## System rules

- Build around loud display versus calm body; that contrast is the defining typographic device.
- Let the heading face bridge expression and utility.
- Use scale and weight for impact; avoid ornate letterforms as the primary source of loudness.
- Preserve generous line height and predictable spacing in body copy.
- Verify language and script coverage before selecting a family.
- Test long labels, localization expansion, numerals, punctuation, mixed case, and text scaling.
- Do not make display, heading, body, and metadata roles equally intense.
